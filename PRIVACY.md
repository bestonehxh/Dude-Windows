# Dude for Windows — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

Dude does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Your labels stay on your PC.** Rows and label settings are saved only in `%APPDATA%\Dude` on your
  computer. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Printer connection.** Dude talks to your Brother PT-P710BT over Bluetooth or USB only to read
  its status and send the labels you print.
- **Files.** Dude reads or writes a CSV file only when you choose one in Import or Export.
- **No analytics, tracking or advertising.**
- **Updates.** Dude checks GitHub for a newer release about once a day. That request contains no
  information about you or your labels.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/bestonehxh/Dude-Windows/issues
